FORMULAS
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

RISK SCORE:
  Formula: Likelihood × Impact × Velocity × Detectability
  Range: 1.0 (minimum) to 625.0 (maximum)
  Example: 5 × 5 × 5 × 5 = 625.0

NORMALISED SCORE:
  Formula: round(risk_score / 6.25, 1)
  Range: 0.2 to 100.0
  Example: 625 / 6.25 = 100.0

COLOUR BAND:
  if score >= 65: Critical (red)
  else if >= 40: High (orange)
  else if >= 20: Medium (amber)
  else: Low (green)

INSTITUTIONAL RISK INDEX:
  Formula: Σ(linked_scores) × avg(weights) × sqrt(departments)
  Purpose: Measure institutional impact of risk cluster
